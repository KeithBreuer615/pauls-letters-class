Lesson 2

Saul Before Paul

Grace redirects Saul’s identity, confidence, community, and purpose toward Jesus Christ.

Primary textsActs 7:54–9:31
Paul looks backGalatians 1:11–24; Philippians 3:4–11

Follow the transformation

Saul did not slowly improve himself into an apostle. The risen Jesus confronted an opponent, called him by grace, and placed him among the very people he had tried to destroy.

Before Christ

Confidence and opposition

Identify what Saul valued, what he believed he was defending, and how those convictions shaped his actions.

Christ’s initiative

Confronted and called

Watch who acts first in Acts 9 and Galatians 1. Grace interrupts Saul before Saul seeks Christ.

A new community

Received by believers

Notice the roles of Ananias, Barnabas, and the churches. Christ’s call joins Saul to Christ’s people.

A new confidence

Knowing Christ

In Philippians 3, compare Saul’s former grounds for confidence with what he now counts most valuable.

Central question

What in these passages shows that Saul’s transformation is an act of grace rather than a project of self-improvement?

Discuss

  1. What did Saul value before meeting Christ, and how had those values directed his life?
  2. Where do you see God’s initiative in the Damascus account and in Paul’s own retelling?
  3. How does the church participate in Saul’s new life and calling?
  4. What changes—and what abilities or experiences does Christ redirect rather than erase?

Before Lesson 3

Paul’s Calling and Mission

Read Acts 9:1–31; Acts 13:1–12; Romans 15:14–33.
