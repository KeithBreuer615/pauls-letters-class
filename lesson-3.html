Lesson 3

Paul’s Calling and Mission

The risen Christ called Paul, and the Spirit sent him through the church to carry the gospel to the Gentiles and build up Christ’s people.

BeginningActs 9:1–31
Sending and purposeActs 13:1–12; Romans 15:14–33

Called by Christ, sent through the church

Paul’s mission begins with Christ’s authority, is confirmed in a worshiping congregation, depends on the Spirit’s direction, and aims to gather people who belong to Jesus.

Acts 9

Christ defines the mission

Trace what Paul is called to proclaim, whom he is sent to serve, and what suffering will accompany his work.

Acts 13

The church sends

Notice worship, prayer, fasting, the Spirit’s direction, and the congregation’s commissioning.

Romans 15

Paul explains his ambition

Let Paul describe the grace entrusted to him, the places he has served, and the work still ahead.

A shared mission

Congregations participate

Mission includes going, sending, praying, giving, welcoming, teaching, and growing together as Christ’s people.

Central question

How do Christ’s call, the Spirit’s direction, and the church’s participation fit together without competing with one another?

Discuss

  1. What parts of Paul’s calling are unique to his apostolic ministry?
  2. What does the Antioch church teach us about discerning and supporting mission?
  3. According to Romans 15, what results does Paul expect the gospel to produce?
  4. How can a congregation participate faithfully even when most members are not sent across cultures?

Before Lesson 4

Galatians, Part 1

Read Galatians 1–6 in one sitting, then read Acts 15. Listen for the problem Paul addresses and the gospel truth he protects.
